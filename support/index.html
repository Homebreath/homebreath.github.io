Support

Questions, ideas, or something that doesn't work as it should.

Write to homebreath@outlook.com. Homebreath is made by one person, so an answer can take a few days, but every message is read.

If something doesn't work, it helps to mention your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when it happened.

Where are my visits and notes kept?

Everything you write in Homebreath, and the photo you add of your own home, stays on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Nobody else can read it, including us.

Will my entries come with me to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you move to a new iPhone from a backup of your old one (iCloud or a computer), or transfer directly from your old iPhone. If you delete the app without a backup, your entries are gone.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Open My House and tap Reminders and settings. Reminders are off until you turn them on.

How do I delete what I wrote?

In Reminders and settings you can delete all your visits. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on your iPhone.
