Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Homebreath does not collect what you write. Your entries and the photo you add stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. If you subscribe, Apple handles the payment and RevenueCat checks whether your subscription is active (see Purchases).

What stays on your iPhone

Your visits, notes, journal entries and answers, and the photo of your home that you choose to add, are stored only inside the app on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, these entries are part of that backup. Backups are handled by Apple under your own Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

Homebreath only asks for the camera or your photo library when you choose to add a photo of your home. The photo stays on your iPhone.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your iPhone itself. No information is sent to us for this.

Purchases

Homebreath offers a monthly and a yearly subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or bank details. To check whether you have an active subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that manages app subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID created on your iPhone, your purchase history from Apple and basic device information such as the app version and country. It does not receive your name or email address, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone settings under your Apple Account.

Apple

When you download Homebreath, Apple processes information about that download under its own privacy policy. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. These do not identify you.

Deleting your entries

In the app, open My House and tap Reminders and settings to delete all your visits, and separately all your notes. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on your iPhone.

Children

Homebreath is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to show, correct or delete. If you write to us, we use your email only to answer you and delete it when it is no longer needed. You can contact us with any privacy question at homebreath@outlook.com, and you have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
